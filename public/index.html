The Story Behind the Shield

About Kalasag Audit

Named after the traditional Filipino kalasag — a carved hardwood battle shield built to protect its bearer — Kalasag Audit is a website security checker that finds real vulnerabilities and misconfigurations without flooding you with false alarms.

Why "Kalasag Audit"?

Most free security scanners overwhelm developers with false warnings — flagging safe Apache headers, mistaking normal 404 pages for leaked .env files, or panicking over blocked IIS headers.

Kalasag Audit combines the heritage concept of a strong outer shield with accurate, verified testing: every warning is double-checked against real web standards and backed by regression tests.

01

Zero External Dependencies

Runs purely on built-in Node.js tools. Fast to launch, lightweight, and completely self-contained.

02

100% Local & Private

Runs right on your own computer (127.0.0.1). Safely test localhost apps and staging servers without sending data to the cloud.

03

Verified Findings, Not Guesses

Tests actually confirm whether files can be written or deleted, ignore HTML 404 pages, and clean up after themselves.

04

Fair & Honest Grading

Small minor issues won't ruin a secure site's score, while real critical leaks always cap the grade until fixed.

How It Inspects a Website

Five Security Layers Checked in Under Two Seconds

Every scan inspects your website from the outside in — just like a security researcher would — without touching your database or crashing your server.

  • Layer 1 — Domain & Email (DNS): Checks SPF, DMARC, CAA, MX, DKIM, and dangling subdomains that attackers could hijack.
  • Layer 2 — Connection & SSL: Verifies HTTPS redirects, modern TLS 1.2/1.3 encryption, strong ciphers, and valid certificate expiry dates.
  • Layer 3 — Browser Protection Headers: Inspects HSTS, Content Security Policy (CSP), Clickjacking protection, MIME-sniffing defense, and Referrer rules.
  • Layer 4 — Sessions & Access Rules: Reviews cookie security flags (Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite), CORS rules, and dangerous HTTP methods.
  • Layer 5 — Leaked Files & Frontend Scripts: Safely checks for accidentally public .git or .env files, mixed HTTP content, outdated JS libraries, Open Redirects, and optional OTP spam protection.

How Grades Work

Minor issues have diminishing point deductions so small cosmetic notes never ruin a strong site, while serious risks always cap your maximum grade until fixed:

CRITICALFix immediately • Caps grade at D (max 40)
HIGHHigh priority • Caps grade at B (max 84)
MEDIUMRecommended fix • Caps grade at A (max 94)
LOWGood polish • Minor point adjustment, no grade cap

Ethical & Safe Use Charter

Kalasag Audit is built strictly for white-hat security checks, pre-launch QA, and helping teams protect their own websites.

  • Only Scan Authorized Sites: Test apps you own, staging environments, or programs where you have explicit permission.
  • Safe & Non-Destructive: Never runs destructive attacks, SQL injection, or password guessing.
  • Share Fixes Privately: Use the Markdown or PDF export to hand clear remediation steps directly to developers.
